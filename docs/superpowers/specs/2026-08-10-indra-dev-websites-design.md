# INDRA.DEV Portfolio and Technical Blog Design

**Date:** 2026-08-10  
**Status:** Approved design; implementation not started  
**Applications:** `/home/mahad/code/Personal/portfolio` and `/home/mahad/code/Personal/blog`

## 1. Objective

Build two separate, consistent websites under the `INDRA.DEV` identity:

- A recruiter-focused portfolio positioning Mahadi Indra Manurung as a Senior Frontend Engineer in Indonesia.
- A technical blog for software engineers covering React, Next.js, TypeScript, React Native, and production-oriented code examples.

The sites will be deployed as separate Vercel projects and eventually use separate domains. They must remain visually related while clearly identifying whether the visitor is on the portfolio or blog.

## 2. Success Criteria

### Portfolio

- A recruiter can identify the candidate's role, experience level, primary domain experience, and CV action within ten seconds.
- Work experience communicates ownership and impact rather than presenting an undifferentiated technology list.
- Projects and technology choices are contextualized inside the relevant work experience.
- The latest blog content reinforces technical credibility without distracting from the recruiting path.

### Blog

- Engineers can browse by topic or series, search content, and reach an article quickly.
- Long-form content, code blocks, and mobile reading remain comfortable and accessible.
- Posts can be generated by Hermes through a validated content contract without making the production build fragile.
- Indonesian and English variants use stable locale URLs and explicit translation relationships.

### Shared

- Header, footer, theme behavior, language control, typography, colors, spacing, and motion feel identical.
- Both light and dark themes pass WCAG AA contrast requirements.
- The experience is usable at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixel viewport widths.

## 3. Scope

### MVP

- Two independent Next.js applications using the App Router and TypeScript.
- Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui primitives.
- Shared visual contract implemented consistently in both applications.
- Light and dark user-selectable themes with the operating-system theme as the first-visit default.
- Indonesian and English locale routing.
- Portfolio content derived from the supplied CV and rewritten for web scanning.
- MDX blog content with schema validation.
- Search, topic and series filters, load more, table of contents, syntax highlighting, copy-code, reading time, related posts, share controls, RSS, sitemap, Open Graph metadata, and structured data.
- Latest blog posts shown on the portfolio through a public feed.
- Responsive, accessible states and focused automated tests.

### Later Phase

- Hermes publishing automation scheduled for 20:00 Asia/Jakarta.
- Translation generation or editorial assistance for paired Indonesian and English posts.
- Additional case-study depth and measurable project outcomes as approved information becomes available.
- Analytics, comments, and newsletter only after a clear product need is established.

### Explicitly Excluded from MVP

- Analytics.
- Comment system.
- Newsletter subscription.
- Live code playground.
- CMS dashboard or multi-author publishing.
- Decorative 3D, scroll-jacking, animated cursors, and heavy parallax.

## 4. Visual Direction

### Style

The approved direction is **Authority Editorial**: a strict editorial grid, clear hierarchy, high-contrast typography, restrained blue accents, and credibility-first content. The design avoids purple or pink gradients, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, random glow effects, emoji icons, and other generic AI-generated visual patterns.

### Brand

- Primary wordmark: `INDRA.DEV` in uppercase.
- The dot is the repeatable accent detail and may use the brand blue.
- Favicon: compact `ID` monogram designed for legibility at 16, 24, and 32 pixels.
- Profile photography: use the supplied portrait with an editorial crop. Preserve natural proportions and do not apply AI retouching or decorative background replacement.

### Color Tokens

Light theme:

- Background: `#FAFAFA`
- Foreground: `#101114`
- Surface: `#F1F3F6`
- Muted text: `#5D626B`
- Border: `#D8DADD`
- Accent: `#2563EB`
- Accent foreground: `#FFFFFF`
- Destructive: `#DC2626`

Dark theme:

- Background: `#0B0D10`
- Foreground: `#F5F7FA`
- Surface: `#15181D`
- Muted text: `#A6ADB8`
- Border: `#2C3139`
- Accent: `#60A5FA`
- Accent foreground: `#07111F`
- Destructive: `#F87171`

All component styling must consume semantic variables rather than hardcoded theme-specific colors.

### Typography

- Primary interface and editorial typeface: Inter Variable.
- Code and technical metadata: Geist Mono.
- Body text starts at 16 pixels with a 1.6 to 1.75 line height.
- Article measure is limited to approximately 65 to 75 characters.
- Headings use weight, spacing, and scale for hierarchy rather than decorative treatment.

### Layout

- Mobile-first layout with a consistent four or eight pixel spacing rhythm.
- Portfolio may use a wider editorial grid; article prose remains in a narrow reading column.
- Desktop maximum content width is consistent across both sites.
- Fixed or sticky elements reserve space and never cover scrollable content.
- There must be no horizontal overflow at supported viewport sizes.

### Icons

- Use one SVG icon family consistently through shadcn-compatible components.
- Icons never replace required accessible text without an `aria-label` and tooltip.
- Interactive icon targets are at least 44 by 44 pixels.
- The theme control includes only sun and moon icons. The first visit follows the operating-system theme internally; System is not rendered as a menu option or icon.

## 5. Motion

Motion is subtle and functional:

- Hero and primary content blocks may enter with opacity and an 8 to 12 pixel vertical offset over 300 to 420 milliseconds.
- Hover and pressed transitions run for 150 to 200 milliseconds.
- Experience rows may reveal a surface change and small arrow translation without changing surrounding layout.
- Theme transitions may crossfade colors but must not delay interaction.
- Only opacity and transform are used for primary animations.
- No more than one or two motion groups appear in a viewport.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` removes entrance and nonessential transitions.
- Content remains visible and usable when JavaScript is unavailable.

The MVP should use CSS transitions and small framework-native effects. GSAP is not required.

## 6. Shared Navigation and Footer

### Footer

Both sites use the same footer:

- `INDRA.DEV` wordmark.

## 9. Content Model

### Portfolio Data

Portfolio data is typed local content rather than component-embedded copy. At minimum it models:

- Profile and positioning.
- Localized about copy.
- Employers or engagements.
- Nested projects.
- Role, period, responsibilities, impact, and technologies.
- Testimonials with author, role, organization, quote, and approval state.
- External profile and contact links.

### Blog MDX Frontmatter

Every post must include:

- `title`
- `slug`
- `description`
- `locale`
- `translationKey`
- `publishedAt`
- `updatedAt` when applicable
- `topics`
- `series` and `seriesOrder` when applicable
- `cover` when applicable
- `draft`
- Canonical and social metadata when they differ from generated defaults

Reading time is derived during content processing rather than authored manually. Slugs are unique within a locale. A shared `translationKey` links locale variants.

## 10. Localization

- Default locale is Indonesian.
- Public URLs use explicit `/id` and `/en` prefixes for stable SEO and alternate links.
- Interface translations and content translations are separate resources.
- The locale switch maps to the same portfolio page or translated article using route identity or `translationKey`.
- If an article has no target-language translation, the switch goes to the target-language blog index and displays a nonblocking explanation.
- Dates use the active locale and Asia/Jakarta semantics for publication scheduling.
- `hreflang`, canonical URLs, and localized Open Graph metadata are generated per route.

## 11. Theme Behavior

- First visit follows the operating-system theme.
- User selection supports Light and Dark modes.
- Explicit selection is persisted locally.
- When no explicit selection has been persisted, the default continues following the operating-system theme.
- Initial server and client rendering must avoid a visible incorrect-theme flash.
- Theme changes do not reset scroll position, filters, language, or article state.

## 12. Cross-Site Blog Feed

The blog exposes a public latest-post feed containing only published metadata required by the portfolio. The portfolio requests the localized feed using a configurable blog base URL and an hourly revalidation interval.

- Successful revalidation updates Latest Writing without a manual portfolio deployment.
- If revalidation fails after a previous success, the last generated portfolio page remains available through incremental static regeneration behavior.
- If no feed has ever been available, the portfolio displays a direct Blog link rather than an empty or broken card grid.
- Feed failures never block the rest of the portfolio page.
- The feed version and schema are validated to prevent incompatible content from entering rendering.

## 13. Hermes Publishing Contract

Hermes automation is a later-phase publishing integration, not part of the website MVP implementation. Its required contract is defined now:

1. Produce one or two locale-specific MDX files using the approved frontmatter schema.
2. Set the intended publication date using Asia/Jakarta semantics.
3. Run formatting, frontmatter validation, unique-slug checks, link checks, and a production build.
4. Keep failed content as draft and report a recoverable error rather than publishing it.
5. Commit valid content through an approved repository workflow.
6. Allow the blog's Vercel deployment to publish the result.
7. Allow the portfolio feed revalidation to surface the new post within approximately one hour.

Scheduling is configured for 20:00 Asia/Jakarta only when the external Hermes workflow is implemented and explicitly authorized.

## 14. Loading, Empty, and Error States

- Reserve media and asynchronous content dimensions to keep cumulative layout shift below 0.1.
- Use lightweight skeletons only where loading exceeds approximately 300 milliseconds.
- Search or filter with no results explains the active constraint and offers a reset action.
- Invalid or missing posts return a localized not-found page.
- Blog feed failure falls back according to the cross-site feed rules.
- Copy-code success uses a short status change announced through a polite live region.
- Download CV failure retains a visible direct link and does not rely on a toast alone.
- All errors state what happened and the next available action.

## 15. Accessibility and Performance

- WCAG 2.2 AA color contrast for text and controls.
- Semantic landmarks, sequential headings, skip link, descriptive image alternatives, and visible focus rings.
- Full keyboard navigation and predictable focus movement for menus and route changes.
- Minimum 44 pixel pointer targets and at least eight pixels between adjacent compact targets.
- No primary interaction depends only on hover, motion, or color.
- Profile and article images use responsive Next.js image behavior with declared dimensions or aspect ratio.
- Font loading uses `next/font` with layout-stable fallbacks.
- Code highlighting is performed at build or server time where practical to avoid a large client bundle.
- Client components are limited to interactions that require browser state.

## 16. Testing and Verification

### Automated

- Unit tests for portfolio content types, MDX schema, slug uniqueness, locale mapping, theme preference logic, feed schema, and load-more filtering.
- Integration tests for blog feed generation and portfolio feed consumption, including malformed and unavailable responses.
- Playwright coverage for Download CV, theme selection, system-theme behavior, locale switching, mobile navigation, search, filtering, load more, article navigation, copy-code, and feed fallback.

### Manual

- Responsive review at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixels, including landscape where relevant.
- Keyboard-only navigation and screen-reader spot checks.
- Light, dark, system, and reduced-motion modes.
- Loading, empty, unavailable-feed, not-found, and invalid-content states.
- Lighthouse review for accessibility, performance, SEO, and best practices.

## 17. Deployment

- Portfolio and blog are separate Vercel projects with separate root directories and environment configuration.
- No analytics scripts are installed in the MVP.
- Each site generates its own sitemap, robots directives, Open Graph images, canonical URLs, and structured data.
- Domain-specific values are environment-driven until final domains are purchased.
- Preview deployments are used for visual and content review before production promotion.

## 18. Implementation Boundaries

- The application folders remain separate deployables.
- Consistency is governed by an explicit shared design contract and parity tests rather than an externally published UI package in the MVP.
- The implementation plan must establish one canonical token and component specification first, then apply and verify the same contract in both applications.
- No dependencies are added unless they directly support the approved capabilities and their purpose is recorded in the implementation plan.
- Existing or future unrelated projects under `/home/mahad/code/Personal` remain untouched.
